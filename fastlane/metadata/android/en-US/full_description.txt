Kate is an open-source music player for the songs already on your phone. No accounts, no streaming, no ads. Just your library, with great sound, synced lyrics, and a look you can make completely your own.

<b>Looks like yours</b>
• Six player layouts: Classic, Big Art, Minimal, Vinyl, Cassette, and Card
• Five seek bars, including a waveform worked out from each song
• A visualizer behind the player, in the mini player, or behind the lyrics
• Dozens of accents, Material You, or colors taken from the album art
• Build your own themes and styles with a live preview, and share them as a file
• Your own fonts, button styles, icons, and text size

<b>Sound</b>
• Equalizer with presets, bass and treble boost, and a parametric Advanced EQ with AutoEQ profiles
• A separate EQ for the speaker, wired headphones, Bluetooth, and USB audio
• EQ that follows the music: link one to a song, album, folder, or genre
• Crossfeed, dynamics with a night mode, loudness compensation, and a limiter
• ReplayGain, crossfade, gapless albums, and speed changes that keep the pitch
• Optional native audio engine (Oboe), hi-res output, and bit-perfect USB output on Android 14 and newer

<b>Lyrics</b>
• Synced lyrics from tags or .lrc files, highlighted word by word
• Optional online lyrics search, saved into the song so they work offline
• Make your own synced lyrics by tapping along as the song plays
• Share any lines as a lyric card image

<b>Library</b>
• You pick the folders, so SD cards just work
• Browse by songs, albums, artists, genres, folders, years, and recently added
• Smart playlists, plus M3U import and export
• Tag editor with optional online lookup

<b>Playing and stats</b>
• Six shuffle modes and Kate Radio, which keeps going with similar songs from your library
• Top songs, artists, albums, and genres, plus a Wrapped story for any week, month, or year
• Achievements like Night Owl and Album Finisher
• Listening history stays on your device, and you can turn it off or clear it

<b>Everywhere else</b>
• Four home screen widgets and Android Auto
• Headset button actions for single, double, triple, and long press
• Two-pane layout on tablets, foldables, and wide screens
• Backup and restore for your settings, playlists, favorites, and history

Kate needs Android 8.0 or newer.
